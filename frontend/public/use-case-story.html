Maria, in Porto, Portugal,
sends €500 to her brother Aram in Yerevan, Armenia.
Step 1–3 · the money leaves Maria's bank
Maria pays €500 in-app
Bison Bank / Bison Digital Assets — Portugal's first MiCA-licensed bank CASP, PSD2 + MiCA authorised
KYC / AML / sanctions check runs
Automated, against the sender and recipient on file
⚓ HCS ANCHOR · initiation
Compliance record + FX quote hashed & anchored
Anyone can verify this passed, at this moment, on a public Mirror Node
Step 4–5 · choosing the rail
Router checks real liquidity depth
Live pull from SaucerSwap, Hedera's native DEX — not a hardcoded number
⚓ HCS ANCHOR · routing
Decision: settle on Hedera, in EURC
€500 is within the safe threshold; the reason itself is hashed and anchored, not just asserted
Why a two-signature account, not our wallet?
EURC settles into a Hedera account governed by a threshold key — 2 of 2 signatures required. One key belongs to Bison Bank, one to the destination CASP. Neither key is ours.
We never hold a private key here. This is the same non-custodial promise, just made structurally true.
Step 6–8 · the money arrives
Destination CASP co-signs the release
A CBA-licensed crypto-asset service provider, Armenia — both keys sign, EURC moves out
EURC → AMD, credited to Aram's account
Ordinary bank account, or mobile wallet where that's the norm. Aram never sees a blockchain.
Both partners sign a Schedule
Hedera Schedule Service · a pending transaction, waiting on exactly these two signatures
⚓ HCS ANCHOR · completion
Network auto-executes the moment both sign
"This transfer completed, end to end" — anchored the instant it's true, by no one's clock but Hedera's
Three anchors. One verifiable story.
Initiation, routing, completion — each independently checkable on a public Mirror Node, by Maria, by Aram, by a regulator, by no one at all if they'd rather just trust the receipt.
Behind the Scenes
The actual function calls

anchorComplianceRecord() + anchorQuote() — initiation anchor
anchorRoutingDecision() — why this chain, hashed and anchored
executeSettlement() — real HTS transfer into the 2-of-2 account
ScheduleCreateTransaction() + two ScheduleSign calls — completion, gated
verify.mjs — anyone recomputes the hash against the public Mirror Node
For Maria. For Aram. For anyone sending money across a border.
